GRID SCALPER PRO — v2.31
Immediate Grid Engine for MetaTrader 5

A practical grid / mean-reversion EA with explicit risk gates — not an “set and forget forever” black box. Built for XAUUSD and major FX pairs on M1–M15.

WHAT IT DOES
• Opens the first market entry when the side is flat (and filters allow), then adds grid steps at GridDistance (with optional step widening).
• Exit modes: TP_Average (basket TP from volume-weighted average) or TP_Individual (per-position TP from its own entry, optional FixedStopLoss).
• Optional buy/sell price ranges, server-time window, cooldown after entries and closes, MaxDD equity protection (block or close-all).

DUPLICATE & NETTING GUARDS (v2.30–2.31)
• Single in-flight order lock — confirmed by deal / volume, reconciled on timeout (no aggressive re-fire).
• Same-level block: refuses a new entry within GridDistance × EntryGap of the last fill or any open algo price. Critical on netting / equity accounts where a second order at the same level merges and doubles volume.
• Startup recovery from open positions (and netting depth from volume vs LotSequence) so MaxOrders still applies after restart — no “invisible” first re-entry when a side is already live.

SAFETY INPUTS (each switchable)
• MaxDDToHedge / CloseOnMaxDD — dollar drawdown arm; block new entries or close managed positions.
• UseAutoRisk, margin buffer, low margin-level stop.
• UseCooldown + CooldownSeconds after every entry and close.
• Hard skip when symbol spread > 500 points (built-in).

DASHBOARD
Draggable HUD: equity, drawdown, net profit, buy/sell counts and volume. Optional auto light/dark theme.

STARTING POINT
• Gold (XAUUSD): M1/M5 — use the Gold_Optimized.set template (GridDistance 400, EntryGap 1.0, Cooldown on, Average TP) and validate on demo for your broker’s points and margin.
• Forex: wider GridDistance, lower MaxOrders, session time filter as needed.
